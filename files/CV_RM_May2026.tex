\vspace{6pt}
Doctoral researcher working at the intersection of neuromotor control, neural interfaces and machine learning. My work focuses on decoding the neural drive underlying hand movements from spinal motor neuron activity recorded non-invasively, to then develop machine learning models that can predict finger forces and generate commands for external devices. I also have 10 years of experience developing artificial robotic limbs and assistive devices. My past projects include studying human augmentation by controlling an additional robotic finger while playing piano, the design of low-cost bionic hand prostheses (now commercialised), and developing a real-time control system for a gait-assistance exoskeleton.
